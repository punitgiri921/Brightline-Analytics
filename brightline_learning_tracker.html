🧠 The Seven Lenses of Architectural Mastery

At every phase gate, you will be evaluated across all seven lenses before unlocking the next stage.

1. WHAT
Clear, precise technical definition of the concept or component without jargon fluff.
2. WHY
Architectural rationale, enterprise business driver, and the anti-patterns it prevents.
3. HOW
Exact mechanical execution, engine behavior, formulas, and step-by-step lifecycle.
4. DEPENDENCIES
Upstream/downstream prerequisites (gateway, drivers, capacities, OAuth tokens, parameters).
5. PERMISSIONS
Exact security roles (Admin, Member, Contributor, Viewer, Build, SPN, Gateway Admin).
6. LIMITATIONS
Boundary conditions, license restrictions, unsupported data types, and scale limits.
7. TROUBLESHOOTING
Failure symptoms, diagnostic logs, root cause analysis, and surgical recovery steps.
